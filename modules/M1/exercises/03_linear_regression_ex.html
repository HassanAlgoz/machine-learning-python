Exercise: Linear Regression

Note: you can Download the notebook from the sidebar, where it says “Jupyter”.

Your task is to do the following:

  1. Load the data
  2. Identify the features and their types
  3. Identify the target and its type
  4. Explore the data
    • What is the number of samples?
    • What is the distribution of the features? (min, max, mean, median, std, quantiles). Hint: use DataFrame.describe()
    • What is the distribution of the target?
    • Are there missing values?
  5. What is the relationship between the feature and the target? Increasing, decreasing, random, or non-linear (changing: maybe, increasing until X, then decreasing)? Plot the feature vs the target to answer this.
  6. Initialize a regression model
  7. Train the model (on the train set)
  8. Evaluate the model (on the test set)
  9. Make predictions on new cases of your choice
